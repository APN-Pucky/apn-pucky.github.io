# Research

My work is in precision predictions for high-energy particle collisions:
combining fixed-order QCD and electroweak calculations with parton-shower
Monte Carlo event generators, with an emphasis on real and virtual photon
production as probes of the quark-gluon plasma and of QCD factorization
more broadly.

<ul class="research-grid">
  <li>
    <h3><a href="{{ '/research/photon-fragmentation/' | relative_url }}">Photon Fragmentation Functions</a></h3>
    <p>Extracting effective parton-to-photon fragmentation functions from PYTHIA and distributing them as LHAPDF grids.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h3><a href="{{ '/research/low-mass-dileptons/' | relative_url }}">Low-Mass Dileptons &amp; Virtual Photons</a></h3>
    <p>Internal conversion of real and virtual photons into lepton pairs, from the Kroll-Wada formula to parton-shower descriptions.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h3><a href="{{ '/research/photon-jet-powheg/' | relative_url }}">Precision Photon+Jet Production</a></h3>
    <p>NLO QCD predictions for prompt photons with jets, matched to parton showers via POWHEG and MiNLO.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h3><a href="{{ '/research/geneva-nnlo-ew/' | relative_url }}">NNLO QCD + NLO EW Drell-Yan</a></h3>
    <p>Combining NNLO QCD with NLO electroweak corrections for Drell-Yan lepton pairs in the GENEVA framework.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h3><a href="{{ '/research/numerical-methods-mc/' | relative_url }}">Numerical Methods for Event Generators</a></h3>
    <p>Adaptive and rejection sampling, Sudakov-factor numerics, and scientific-computing aspects of Monte Carlo generation.</p>
  </li>
</ul>

See also the [full publication list]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}),
