Prompt photon production is highly sensitive to the parton content of the
colliding protons (or nuclei) and serves as a key baseline for
quark-gluon-plasma phenomenology. This research improves predictions for
direct photon production accompanied by QCD jets — in particular photon
production with two jets — using the [POWHEG](https://powhegbox.mib.infn.it/)
method matched to parton showers, extending earlier single-jet
calculations.

Work in this area includes implementing the photon-plus-two-jets process
in POWHEG BOX V2 at next-to-leading order QCD, studying how different
parton-shower models describe photon fragmentation and photon-jet angular
correlations, and comparing predictions against ATLAS data. A related
strand investigates MiNLO-type Sudakov factors for processes with three
colored legs, where the choice of resummation scale and the treatment of
soft wide-angle radiation are not yet settled; this numerical work is
exploratory and is reported here only once results are validated and
public.
