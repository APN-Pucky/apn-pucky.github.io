The [GENEVA](https://geneva.physics.lbl.gov/) framework combines
higher-order QCD resummation with parton-shower matching to provide fully
differentiable, NNLO-accurate predictions matched to parton showers. This
is collaborative research, carried out within the GENEVA collaboration, on
extending Drell-Yan Z/γ\* lepton-pair production to combine NNLO QCD with
NLO electroweak corrections, including photon-induced subprocesses and
initial- and final-state photon radiation off massive charged leptons.

Technical aspects of this work include POWHEG-style radiation mappings for
the electroweak corrections, the choice of electroweak input scheme (e.g.
the G<sub>μ</sub> scheme), interfacing with matrix-element providers such
as OpenLoops and RECOLA, and validating the resulting fixed-order and
shower-matched predictions against existing NNLO QCD and NLO electroweak
calculations.

This is ongoing, collaborative work; results will be linked here once
published, and are not attributed solely to any one contributor.
