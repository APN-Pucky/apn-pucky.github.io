Low-invariant-mass lepton-pair production is a sensitive observable in both
proton-proton and heavy-ion collisions: dilepton pairs arise from the
internal conversion of real and virtual photons, making them an
experimentally accessible proxy for photon production itself, and a probe
of the quark-gluon plasma. The classic Kroll-Wada formalism and the related
soft-photon / Low-Burnett-Kroll approximations provide a simple, universal
description of this conversion, but their accuracy away from the strict
soft limit is not guaranteed.

This research compares the Kroll-Wada approach with an explicit
parton-shower treatment of virtual-photon emission and conversion, and
separately quantifies the virtual-photon signal against background
contributions from heavy-flavour (charm and bottom) decays in di-electron
final states, relevant for measurements such as those from PHENIX, ALICE
and STAR.
