description: >-
  Doctoral thesis combining fixed-order QCD calculations with parton showers
  via the POWHEG method to compute the production of real and virtual
  photons at the LHC, both of which serve as signals of the quark-gluon
  plasma, together with studies of BSM particle production. Includes the
  implementation of a new POWHEG BOX V2 process for photon production with
  two jets at next-to-leading order QCD.
