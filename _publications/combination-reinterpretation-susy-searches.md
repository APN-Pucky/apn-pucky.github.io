description: >-
  To maximise the information obtained from independent new-physics searches
  at the LHC, it is imperative to consider the combination of multiple
  analyses. We examine a simplified supersymmetry scenario with one squark
  flavour and a bino-like neutralino, combining strong squark pair
  production, associated squark-neutralino production, and weak neutralino
  pair production. Combining these production mechanisms yields stronger
  mass limits than any individual analysis, while weak production is found
  to contribute only marginally to current exclusion bounds.
