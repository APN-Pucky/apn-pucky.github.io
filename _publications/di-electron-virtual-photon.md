description: >-
  The production of virtual photons is a sensitive probe of the properties
  of the quark-gluon plasma. Experimentally they are detected via lepton
  pairs, which suffer from a large background arising from hadron decays. We
  calculate direct contributions from virtual photons produced via the
  Drell-Yan process with an additional jet using POWHEG, finding significant
  effects at low invariant masses, and simulate background contributions
  from charm- and bottom-quark pair production, quantifying theoretical
  uncertainties from scale variations and parton distribution functions. We
  find larger uncertainties for charm- than for bottom-quark contributions.
