description: >-
  We perform a threshold resummation calculation for the associated
  production of squarks and electroweakinos at the LHC to
  next-to-leading-logarithmic (NLL) accuracy, presenting analytical results
  for the soft anomalous dimensions and hard matching coefficients and
  combining the resummed results with fixed-order NLO QCD predictions
  accounting for non-universal squark masses and mixings. Numerically, NLL
  contributions enhance the NLO cross section by 2-6% for squark masses
  between 1-3 TeV, while reducing the scale dependence from about ±10% to
  below ±5%. The calculation is implemented in the public program Resummino.
