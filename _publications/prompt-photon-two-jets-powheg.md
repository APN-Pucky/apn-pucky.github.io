description: >-
  Prompt photon production is highly sensitive to the distribution of quarks
  and gluons in free protons and nuclei and is an important baseline for
  phenomenological studies of the quark-gluon plasma. We present a
  next-to-leading order QCD calculation for prompt photons produced
  alongside two jets, implemented via the POWHEG method and matched to
  parton shower simulations, extending prior single-jet work. We study how
  different parton showers describe photon fragmentation and photon-jet
  correlations, and compare with ATLAS data and higher-order calculations,
  finding that both shower models improve agreement with observations.
