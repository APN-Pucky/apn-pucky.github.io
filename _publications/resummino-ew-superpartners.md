description: >-
  We update the documentation of Resummino, a public program for precision
  calculations of slepton and electroweakino pair production, and explore
  threshold resummation corrections at next-to-next-to-leading-logarithmic
  accuracy for LHC collisions up to 13.6 TeV in simplified models. Results
  show slightly increased total cross sections accompanied by a significant
  decrease of the associated theoretical uncertainties, with additional
  investigation of the squark-mass dependence of the corrections.
