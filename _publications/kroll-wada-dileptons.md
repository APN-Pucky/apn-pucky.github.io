description: >-
  Low-invariant-mass dilepton production is a sensitive probe of the
  properties of the quark-gluon plasma, accessed experimentally through the
  internal conversion of direct and virtual photons into lepton pairs. We
  compare the traditional Kroll-Wada formalism for this conversion with a
  parton-shower-based description, finding that the parton shower approach
  offers improved accuracy in describing the dilepton invariant-mass
  spectrum, particularly towards larger invariant masses where phase-space
  suppression effects become relevant.
